# Flight 13 — Milestone 7: Carry-on Items, Economy and the Hotel Packing Scene

**Goal (user request, 2026-09-24):** a pregame packing area — you pack three items into your carry-on in
a hotel room (Sons of the Forest inventory feel: toss the bag on the bed, it opens, items lie around it
and you pick three). Items come from developer codes, wins, achievements and flight credits. Items work
at night, by day, or any time (even while buckled). Followed by M8 (boarding cutscenes) and M9 (ending
cutscenes).

**Decisions (user):** roles are revealed before packing. Items: Antidote, Defuser, Seatbelt extender,
Pocket flashlight, Sleeping pills, Compact mirror, Frequent-flyer card, Neck pillow, Bobby pin.
Credits: passengers +100 win, +50 alive at landing, Nurse +10 per rescue; saboteurs +10 per kill,
+100 win.

**Architecture:** the engine owns packing and items (host-authoritative, deterministic, tested). New
phases `packing` → `boarding` → `takeoff`. The economy lives in each browser (`src/meta/`): credits,
item counts, achievements, redeemed codes (stored as SHA-256 hashes so the public repo does not reveal
them), settled game ids. The 3D world gains "sets" that share the cabin's renderer and post-processing:
the hotel room is the first.

## Task 1: Engine — items, packing, stats and awards (test-first)
- Types: `ItemId`, phases `packing`/`boarding`, `PlayerState.items/usedItems/poisonedBy`, night
  `asleep/mirrors/flashlights/freed/defused`, day `doubled`, `Bomb.defused`, `GameState.id/packed/stats/awards`,
  intents `pack` and `use`.
- `items.ts`: item info, `checkItemUse`, `possibleItemUses`, `useItem`; automatic items (antidote,
  neck pillow, bobby pin) hook into night resolution.
- Awards at game end per the credit table; views expose items, legal uses, awards and stats.
- Bots pack and use items. Old saves normalize.

## Task 2: Economy (`src/meta/`)
- Bag storage per identity with a starter kit; Duty Free prices; buying; codes; achievements; win
  souvenir; settling a finished game once; deducting used items as they are used.
- Duty Free page (`#/duty-free`): shop, your bag, achievements, redeem a code.

## Task 3: TV
- Carry-on panel (use items, with seat/neighbour pickers), 2D packing screen, boarding card, role card
  at packing, earnings on the end screen.

## Task 4: Hotel packing set (3D)
- Room, bed, lamps, window; the carry-on is tossed onto the bed, unzips and opens; owned items lie
  around it; hover lifts and names an item, click packs it into one of three slots (click again to
  unpack). At the end the bag closes and the view fades to black. Packing HUD over the canvas.

## Task 5: Verify and ship
- Tests, type-check, build; browser: packing with and without items, each item in play, earnings and
  achievements, Duty Free, codes; commit, merge, push, deploy check.
