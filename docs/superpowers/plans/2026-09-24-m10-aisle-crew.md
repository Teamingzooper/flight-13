# M10: Aisle crew, washroom and drink cart

**Goal:** The Stewardess works the aisle instead of a seat. The washroom becomes a once-per-flight hiding place. The drink cart rolls with the Stewardess and blocks the aisle.

## Decisions

**Where the Stewardess is**
- She has no seat. Her position is an aisle spot, stored in `PlayerState.seat` as `"Aisle 5"`.
- `grid.parsePlace` turns seats and aisle spots into cells. Distances, blasts, the Nurse, the handcuffs and whispers therefore reach her like anyone else.
- `parseSeat`, `isSeatInCabin`, `allSeats` and `emptySeats` still mean real seats only, so passengers can never step into the aisle.
- At takeoff the first stewardess starts at row 1 with the cart. Any others spread out behind.
- Everyone can see who the crew are, because they stand in the aisle. Nobody can see which team they are on.

**How she moves and acts**
- Each night she walks to any row, up or down. Another stewardess working a row blocks it.
- She acts only on the six seats beside her.
  - Rogue: `serve` poisons one person sitting in her row.
  - Loyal: `check` looks under the three seats on the left (A–C) or right (D–F) and reports at dawn.
- The loyal check misses the Mastermind's bomb. That replaces the old team check and the "passes as a passenger" rule.
- She has no seat to look under.
- A defuser reaches any seat in her row.

**The drink cart**
- The cart follows the first stewardess still in play. It moves once every seat change has resolved.
- If nobody is left to push it, it stays where it is.
- A runaway cart leaves her behind until her next walk.
- Nobody but crew can walk past the cart's row, judged by where the cart stood when the lights went out. Moving into or out of the cart's own row is allowed.
- The lavatory counts as row R+1, so the cart can cut off the washroom for everyone in front of it.

**The washroom**
- Once per flight, any player can pick `move: 'washroom'` instead of changing seats.
  - Only one person fits each night. If several try, one gets in at random and the others keep their trip.
  - A buckled player cannot go.
  - The lavatory must not be destroyed.
  - The cart must not be in the way.
- A washroom trip:
  - washes out poison straight away;
  - puts you out of reach of drinks, handcuffs, treatment and sleeping pills;
  - makes a blast at your empty seat miss you, though a lavatory bomb still gets you;
  - keeps your seat for you.
- In there you can search the lavatory (answered at once), plant a lavatory bomb if you carry one, or wait.
- A defuser works on a lavatory bomb you found.
- Everyone sees you go: there's a public log line, and the seat map shows your seat as away and your name on the WC.

## Implementation map

**Engine**
- `grid.ts`: `aisleSpot`, `aisleRow`, `parsePlace`, `rowSeats`, `cartBlocks`, `placesWithin`.
- `rules.ts`: `checkMove` covers crew, the washroom and the cart. Also `checkWashroom`, `possibleMoves`, and the new `serve`/`check` rules.
- `night.ts`: `resolveMoves` handles washroom claims and the cart following the stewardess. `resolveNight` handles checks and distance-based blasts.
- Tests: `crew.test.ts`.

**TV**
- `SeatMap` shows a crew badge in the aisle, aisle spots you can pick, and a WC you can tap and that shows who is inside.
- `ActionTab` has the crew move panel, `WashroomCard`, `RowCheckPicker` and `WashroomNight`.

**3D**
- `layout.ts`: crew stand `CREW_BEHIND` the cart and use the `TABLET` on it.
- `Cart` carries the tablet.
- `controls.aisleScript` and a crew path in `boardScript`.
- `Actor.crew` gives a standing, cart-pushing pose, and a dead stewardess ends up on the floor. `goAway`/`comeBack` handle washroom trips.
- `scene/lavatory.ts` builds the interior, which is drawn only while you are in it.
- `searchRoom()` is the lavatory search.
